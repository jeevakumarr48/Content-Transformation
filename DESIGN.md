# TransformIQ Design System

## Direction

Ink + Signal: an editorial intelligence workspace for people who need to move existing information into new contexts without losing its meaning.

## Palette

- Ink: `#202321` for navigation, pipeline surfaces, and high-trust contrast.
- Paper: `#F3F1EC` for the application canvas.
- White: `#FFFEF9` for working surfaces and source documents.
- Cobalt: `#3155D8` for actions, evidence links, and active reasoning states.
- Lime: `#B8DB55` for verified, ready, and demo states.
- Peach: `#F2B09A` for quality and attention metrics.

## Type

DM Sans handles the operating interface. Space Mono is reserved for provenance, locations, status, and system labels. Source content uses a restrained serif treatment to distinguish authored output from controls.

## Composition

The primary surface is an editorial cockpit: a compact ink sidebar, quiet paper canvas, asymmetric evidence panels, and a dark pipeline card that makes the product mechanism visible before generation. Content DNA and claim ledgers use dense rows rather than decorative metric cards. Transformation Studio keeps controls sticky on the left and the generated document, scores, storyboard, verification, and assistant in one scrollable output rail.

## Interaction

Source ingestion is always a distinct state from analysis. Claims, map nodes, verification rows, and generated sentences are selectable and drive the evidence panel. Batch outputs become tabs, and local edits remain explicitly marked as local until audited again.
